# Internal Mobility and Promotion Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-129
Version: 2.3
Last Updated: 2026-06-02
Department: Talent Management
Owner: Head of Talent Management
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Internal Transfers
Employees may apply for internal roles after 12 months in their current role with a rating of 3.5 or higher. The current manager is informed but cannot block a transfer beyond 30 days. Transfers take effect within 60 days.

## 2. Promotions
Promotions are decided in the June and December calibration cycles. A candidate must show sustained performance at the next level for 2 consecutive cycles and be approved by the calibration panel.
