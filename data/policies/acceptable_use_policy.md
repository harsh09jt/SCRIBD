# Acceptable Use of Technology Policy
Document ID: POL-IT-030
Version: 4.2
Last Updated: 2026-04-27
Department: Information Technology
Owner: Chief Information Officer
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Appropriate Use
Company devices, email, networks and cloud services are provided for business use. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work, security or the law.

## 2. Prohibited Activities
Users must not install unlicensed or unapproved software, bypass security controls, access illegal or explicit content, share their accounts, or use personal email or storage for company data.

## 3. Monitoring
Acme may monitor systems and communications for security and compliance to the extent permitted by local law. Users should have no expectation of privacy on company systems.
