# Budgeting and Capital Expenditure Policy
Document ID: POL-FIN-114
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2026-04-01
Department: Global Finance
Owner: VP of Financial Planning & Analysis
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Annual Budget
Budgets are prepared each October and approved by the Board in December. Spending beyond the approved budget by more than 5% needs CFO approval.

## 2. Capital Expenditure
Purchases of assets above $25,000 USD are capitalised and need a business case. Projects above $500,000 USD need Investment Committee approval and a post-implementation review within 12 months.
