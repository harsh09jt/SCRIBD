# Workplace Violence Prevention Policy
Document ID: POL-EHS-003
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2026-02-12
Department: Corporate Security
Owner: Head of Corporate Security
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Zero Tolerance
Threats, intimidation, physical violence and possession of weapons on company premises are prohibited. This includes threats made by phone, email or social media.

## 2. Reporting and Response
Any threat must be reported at once to Corporate Security, and to emergency services if there is immediate danger. Security assesses each report within 4 hours and can restrict site access while it is reviewed.
