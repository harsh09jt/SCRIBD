# Drug and Alcohol-Free Workplace Policy
Document ID: POL-EHS-002
Version: 2.0
Last Updated: 2026-01-14
Department: Environment, Health & Safety
Owner: Head of EHS
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Prohibited Conduct
Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs, and possessing, selling or using illegal drugs on company premises or business, is prohibited. Alcohol may be served only at approved company events.

## 2. Support
Employees who seek help voluntarily before any incident are referred to the EAP and will not be disciplined for asking for support.
