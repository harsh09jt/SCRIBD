# Annual, Sick, Casual and Statutory Leave Policy

Version: 4.0
Last Updated: 2026-01-20
Department: Human Resources
Owner: Head of HR Operations
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Leave Entitlements
- Annual leave: 25 days per year, accruing 2.08 days a month. Up to 10 days may be carried forward and they lapse on March 31.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days.
- Casual leave: 6 days per year.
- Public holidays follow the regional calendar, with a minimum of 10 days.

## 2. Bereavement, Jury Duty and Other Leave
Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for immediate family and 3 days for extended family. Marriage leave is 5 days. Jury duty and court witness time are paid as required by law.

## 3. Applying and Encashment
Apply in the HR portal at least 5 business days ahead for leave longer than 3 days. Managers respond within 2 business days.
Up to 15 unused annual leave days are encashed at separation at the basic daily rate.
