# Environmental Sustainability and ESG Policy
Document ID: POL-ESG-001
Version: 2.3
Last Updated: 2026-07-27
Department: Corporate Sustainability
Owner: Chief Sustainability Officer
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Targets
Acme targets net-zero operational emissions by 2040 and a 50% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030 from a 2022 baseline. Progress is reported annually in the sustainability report.

## 2. Operations
Offices use renewable electricity where available. Business flights are reduced through virtual meetings for trips under 2 hours, and e-waste is recycled through certified vendors.

## 3. Sustainable Investing
ESG risks are included in investment and underwriting decisions for corporate clients.
