# Emergency Evacuation and Crisis Management Policy
Document ID: POL-EHS-004
Version: 2.5
Last Updated: 2026-05-05
Department: Environment, Health & Safety
Owner: Head of EHS
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Evacuation
Every site has an evacuation plan with named floor wardens. Fire drills are held twice a year. On an alarm, employees must leave immediately by the nearest exit and gather at the assembly point.

## 2. Crisis Communication
In a crisis, employees must follow instructions from the Crisis Management Team, check the emergency notification system and not speak to the media.
