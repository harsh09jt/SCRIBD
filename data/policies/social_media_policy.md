# Social Media Policy
Document ID: POL-COM-002
Version: 3.0
Last Updated: 2026-03-04
Department: Corporate Communications
Owner: Head of Corporate Communications
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Personal Use
Employees may use social media personally but must make clear that views are their own, must not share confidential information, customer data or unreleased plans, and must not harass or discriminate.

## 2. Official Accounts
Only trained and approved employees may post on official Acme accounts. Employees must disclose their employment when endorsing Acme products.

## 3. Incidents
A social media post that may damage the company or expose confidential information should be reported to Corporate Communications and Security at once.
