# Travel Safety and Duty of Care Policy
Document ID: POL-EHS-005
Version: 2.2
Last Updated: 2026-06-16
Department: Environment, Health & Safety
Owner: Head of Corporate Security
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Before Travel
International travel must be booked through the approved travel agency so that Acme can locate travellers in an emergency. Travel to high-risk destinations needs Security approval at least 10 business days before departure.

## 2. During Travel
Travellers are covered by the corporate travel insurance and can call the 24/7 assistance line. Employees must check in after arrival in high-risk locations and carry the emergency contact card.
