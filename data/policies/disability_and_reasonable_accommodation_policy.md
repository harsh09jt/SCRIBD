# Disability and Reasonable Accommodation Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-126
Version: 2.2
Last Updated: 2026-02-27
Department: Human Resources & Legal
Owner: Head of Employee Relations
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Requesting an Accommodation
Employees or candidates may request an accommodation from HR at any time. HR starts an interactive discussion within 5 business days and gives a decision within 15 business days.

## 2. Types of Accommodation
Examples include ergonomic or assistive equipment, flexible schedules, remote work, accessible workspaces and adjusted job duties. A denial must be reviewed by Legal.

## 3. Confidentiality
Medical information is kept confidential and stored separately from the personnel file.
