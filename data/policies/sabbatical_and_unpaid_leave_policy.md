# Sabbatical and Extended Unpaid Leave Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-117
Version: 1.8
Last Updated: 2026-03-25
Department: Human Resources
Owner: Total Rewards Director
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Sabbatical Eligibility
Employees with 7 years of continuous service and a rating of 3.5 or higher in the last 2 cycles may take up to 8 weeks of sabbatical at 100% of base pay. Combined with annual leave, the total break may not exceed 12 weeks.

## 2. Extended Unpaid Leave
Up to 6 months of unpaid leave may be granted for education, caregiving or personal circumstances with VP approval.
Health benefits continue for the first 90 days if the employee pays their own contribution.

## 3. Return to Work
Employees return to the same or an equivalent role and must confirm their return date 30 days in advance. Sabbatical pay is repayable pro rata if the employee resigns within 6 months of returning.
