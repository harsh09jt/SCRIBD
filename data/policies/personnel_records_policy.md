# Employee Personnel Records and Privacy Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-130
Version: 2.0
Last Updated: 2026-03-18
Department: Human Resources
Owner: Head of HR Operations
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. What Is Kept
The personnel file contains contracts, role history, performance reviews and payroll details. Medical and disability records are stored separately with restricted access.

## 2. Access and Retention
Employees may request a copy of their own file and receive it within 10 business days. Files are kept for 7 years after separation and then securely destroyed.
