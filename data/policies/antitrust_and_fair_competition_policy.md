# Antitrust and Fair Competition Policy
Document ID: POL-GRC-072
Version: 2.1
Last Updated: 2026-02-24
Department: Governance, Risk & Compliance
Owner: General Counsel
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Prohibited Agreements
Employees must never agree with competitors on prices, discounts, markets, customers, bids or output. Even informal discussions of these topics at trade events are prohibited.

## 2. Trade Associations and Information Sharing
Legal must approve participation in trade association meetings, and an agenda must be circulated in advance. Competitively sensitive information must not be exchanged. Leave the meeting and tell Legal if such topics arise.
