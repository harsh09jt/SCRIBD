# Media Relations and External Communications Policy
Document ID: POL-COM-001
Version: 2.2
Last Updated: 2026-03-04
Department: Corporate Communications
Owner: Head of Corporate Communications
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Authorised Spokespeople
Only the CEO, CFO and Corporate Communications are authorised to speak to the media on behalf of Acme. Employees who receive a media enquiry must forward it to the press office within 1 hour and not comment.

## 2. Speaking Engagements and Publications
External talks, articles and podcasts that mention Acme need approval from Corporate Communications at least 10 business days ahead, and Legal review when confidential topics are involved.
