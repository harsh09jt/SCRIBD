# Fraud Prevention and Investigation Procedure
Document ID: POL-GRC-073
Version: 2.7
Last Updated: 2026-06-25
Department: Governance, Risk & Compliance
Owner: Head of Internal Audit
Access Roles: hr, legal, admin

## 1. Reporting Suspected Fraud
Any suspicion of fraud must be reported to Internal Audit or the ethics hotline within 24 hours. The reporter must not investigate or confront the suspect.

## 2. Investigation Steps
Internal Audit and Legal open a case within 2 business days, secure evidence and interview relevant people. The suspect may be placed on paid leave. Findings are reported to the Audit Committee within 30 days.

## 3. Recovery and Referral
Losses are recovered through payroll deduction, insurance or civil action. Cases involving criminal conduct are referred to law enforcement on the advice of Legal.
