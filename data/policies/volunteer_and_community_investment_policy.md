# Volunteer Time Off and Community Investment Policy
Document ID: POL-CSR-001
Version: 1.7
Last Updated: 2026-04-22
Department: Corporate Social Responsibility
Owner: Head of CSR
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Volunteer Time Off
Each employee receives 16 paid volunteer hours per year for registered non-profit organisations. Hours are logged in the HR portal.

## 2. Donation Matching
Acme matches employee donations to eligible charities 1:1 up to $1,000 USD per employee per year. Political and religious organisations are not eligible.
