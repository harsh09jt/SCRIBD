# Onboarding and Probation Policy
Document ID: POL-HR-131
Version: 2.4
Last Updated: 2026-02-11
Department: Human Resources
Owner: Head of People Operations
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Onboarding
New hires receive laptop and access on day 1, complete a 5-day orientation and agree a 30/60/90-day plan with their manager.

## 2. Probation
Probation lasts 6 months (3 months for interns and contractors). It may be extended once by up to 3 months. Confirmation is issued in writing by HR after a manager review.
