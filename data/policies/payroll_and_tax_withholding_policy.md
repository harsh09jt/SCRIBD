# Payroll Processing and Tax Withholding Policy
Document ID: POL-FIN-113
Version: 3.0
Last Updated: 2026-01-08
Department: Global Finance
Owner: Global Payroll Manager
Access Roles: employee, manager, hr, legal, admin

## 1. Pay Schedule
Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of the month by direct bank transfer. Payslips are available in the HR portal on the day of payment. Changes to bank details must be submitted by the 20th to take effect in that month.

## 2. Tax Withholding
Income tax and statutory contributions are withheld according to local law. Annual tax statements are issued within 30 days of the tax year end.
